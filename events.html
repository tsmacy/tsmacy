South Jersey Events

Updated: 09/21/26

September


🚙 Fri Sep 25 - Sun Sep 27 — “Monsters on the Beach” monster truck beach races on the sand at Spicer Avenue in Wildwood. Excitement kicks off w/show 7 PM Friday.

🍀 Fri Sep 25 - Sun Sep 27 — Annual North Wildwood Irish Fall Festival - Lively music, traditional dance, vendors, parade down Surf Avenue, beer gardens, 5K run, and more.

🚘 Fri Sep 25 - Sun Sep 27 — Wildwood Kustom Hot Rod & Muscle Car Show, on the boardwalk and inside the WWD Conv Ctr. Cars on display from 26th to Burk Avenues and in the convention center.

😁 Sat Sep 26 - Sun Sep 27 — Margate Fall Funfest by the Bay, Saturday 11 AM-6 PM, Sunday 11 AM-5 PM, Amherst Avenue & the Bay. Margate's marquee event features over 110 booths and amusements, unique fine arts and crafts, kids activities, 10 live bands, 12 acts on the kid’s Stage, two days of parades, beer garden, and more.

🍷 Sat Sep 26 — **CANCELED DUE TO WEATHER** Savor September Wine, Beer & Food Festival, Stone Harbor, centered around the 200 block of 96th Street, 11 AM-7 PM, with microbreweries and wineries, tasting tents, food vendors, live music, artisans, and the annual grape stomping competition.
   

October


🍕 Fri Oct 2 - Sat Oct 3 — Olde Tyme Italian Festival at Fox Park in Wildwood, Friday 5-10 PM, Saturday Noon-10 PM. Two-day festival with delicious food, rows of vendors, live music, and more.

🎃 Sat Oct 3 — City of Northfield's Trunk or Treat, 5-7 PM, Birch Grove Park.

🚙 Sat Oct 3 — Ocean City Jeep Invasion, Noon-5 PM, on the Boardwalk between 5th and 14th Streets. 400 Jeeps on display, trophy presentation at 4 PM.

🎃 Sat Oct 17 — Wildwoods Fall Festival, 11 AM-7 PM, Fox Park (across from WWD Conv Ctr), featuring a full day of free family entertainment, food and craft vendors, live bands, a beer garden, and more.

🏃 Sun Oct 18 — John R. Elliott HERO Walk and 1 Mile Fun Run, 9 AM-12:30 PM, to raise awareness about the importance of safe and sober designated drivers. Event begins at Ocean City High School.

🎃 Sat Oct 24 — Halloween fun returns to Cape May County with Boo at the Zoo, 10 AM-2 PM. Festivities for all ages; guests are encouraged to come in costume. Children's games, candy giveaways, live entertainment, animal encounters, an inflatable obstacle course, and more. Costume contest with prizes will start at 11 AM; registration begins at 10:00.

🎃 Sat Oct 24 — Humane Sociey of Ocean City's "Howl-o-Ween" parade, Boardwalk, 2-4 PM. Contestants are encouraged to dress themselves and their pooch in zany, fun Halloween costumes.

🎃 Sat Oct 24 — North Wildwood Trunk-or-Treat Halloween Block Party – Vehicle check-in begins at 1:30 PM at 1st & New Jersey Avenues and the event is 2:30–5:00 along Olde New Jersey Avenue. Music, magic, balloon twisting, and more! Families are encouraged to bring their vehicles decorated for Halloween.

🎃 Sat Oct 24 — 20th Annual Downtown Hammonton Trick or Treat, 2-4 PM. Businesses and organizations throughout downtown (Bellevue Avenue) will hand out candy, plus activities, games, and music.

🎃 Thu Oct 29 — Ocean City Halloween Parade, along Asbury Avenue between 6th and 11th Streets, starts at 7:15 PM.
