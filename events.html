South Jersey Events

Updated: 10/27/26

November


🐎 THROUGHOUT THE MONTH — Horse and Carriage Rides in Ocean City at 9th & Asbury, 12-3 PM: Sat Nov 21, Fri Nov 27, Sat Nov 28, Sun Nov 29


🎃 Sat Nov 14 — "Christmas-ween," a family-friendly, Halloween-meets-Christmas event at Wildwoods Convention Center, 12-4 PM. Costumes welcome, picture opportunities for the whole family, and more. $15 for adults, $10 for children 13 and under.

🎅 Sat Nov 14 — Storybook Land's Christmas Fantasy with Lights opens through December 30th. Over 1 million lights; Santa lights the park at 5 PM each operating day through December 23rd. Tickets must be purchased online.

🚒 Sat Nov 14 — Sea Isle City Fire Department hosts its 135th Anniversary and Dual-Housing Celebration, 1 PM at the Sea Isle City Firehouse at 233 JFK Blvd. Attendees will learn about the Fire Department’s history over the past 135 years, free tours of the firehouse, and get the first glimpses at the town's newest vehicles.

🖼️ Sat Nov 14 — Assumption Regional Catholic School's 36th annual Christmas Craft Fair, 9 AM-5 PM, at the school at 146 S. Pitney Rd. in Galloway



🍽️ Fri Nov 27 - Sun Dec 6 — Wildwoods December Restaurant Week: Showcasing the best of Wildwood's dining scene, with participating restaurants offering special prix fixe breakfast ($15), lunch ($20), and dinner ($45) menus. List of restaurants at WildwoodsRestaurantWeek.com .

🔥 Fri Nov 27 — Family Holiday Bonfire on the Beach, North Wildwood, 2nd & Surf Avenues, 5-7 PM. Bring a beach chair or blanket and enjoy music and free photo ops with Santa. Donation admission fee of $10 for adults, $2 for children 12 & under. Hosted by North Wildwood Volunteer Fire Company #1.

🎅 Sat Nov 28 - Sun Nov 29 — Santa in downtown Ocean City at Soifer Park (9th & Asbury), 9-11 AM

🎅 Sat Nov 28 - Sun Nov 29 — Pictures with Santa at Ocean City Music Pier, 12-3 PM


December


🍽️ Fri Nov 27 - Sun Dec 6 — Wildwoods December Restaurant Week: Showcasing the best of Wildwood's dining scene, with participating restaurants offering special prix fixe breakfast ($15), lunch ($20), and dinner ($45) menus. List of restaurants at WildwoodsRestaurantWeek.com .

🎅 Thu Dec 3 — Folsom's Christmas tree lighting ceremony, Borough Hall, 7 PM. Santa will be there with cookies and cider for kids.

🎄 Fri Dec 4 — Ventnor City's Christmas Tree Lighting ceremony, 5:30 PM, Sacramento Avenue side of City Hall. Enjoy performances by the VECC Rock Band and Margate Community Church Bell Choir.

🎅 Sat Dec 5 — Egg Harbor City Winter Bazaar and Christmas Tree Lighting Ceremony - Bazaar 4-8 PM along Philadelphia Avenue, tree lighting at 7 PM on the 100 block of Philadelphia Avenue. Crafts, games, music, ice skating, pony rides, and more.


🕎 Mon Dec 7 — Ventnor City's Menorah Lighting ceremony, 5:30 PM, Sacramento Avenue side of City Hall

🎅 Sat Dec 12 — Atlantic City's Holiday Parade, starts at 4 PM at New Hampshire & Atlantic Avenues and proceeds down Atlantic. Holiday Village at O'Donnell Park 4-8 PM.



🎅 Sat Dec 19 — Egg Harbor City Christmas Parade, starts at 7 PM. Line-up begins at 6 PM at EHC City Hall.
