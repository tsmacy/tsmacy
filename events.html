🐶 ALL MONTH — Adopt-A-Dog Month: The Atlantic County Animal Shelter is reducing adoption fees for all dogs in its care from $110 to $55, which includes mandatory sterilization, age-appropriate vaccinations, diagnostic testing, a microchip, and free veterinary consultation, in addition to free obedience classes.
